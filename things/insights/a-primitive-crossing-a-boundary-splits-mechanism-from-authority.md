# A Primitive Crossing A Boundary Splits Into Mechanism And Authority

## The Insight

When a primitive that works inside one trust zone is applied across a boundary
between zones, it does not transfer whole. It splits: **the mechanism carries
over; the authority does not.**

The estate month is the worked example. Every intra-domain law extended to the
inter-domain surface, but each shed something at the crossing:

| Primitive | Mechanism that crossed | Authority that did not |
|---|---|---|
| commit-is-real | fetch + fast-forward transport | resolving a divergence; pushing |
| quarantine-then-verify | the pin, the drift check | flipping another domain's `verified` |
| trigger evaluation | reading import state through the face | telling a producer it is watched |
| derived index | the *walk* over things | the *artifact* asserting a membership |

The last row is the crispest. Inside a domain an index is a regenerable
convenience owned by the domain it indexes — it can be wrong only in the way a
cache is wrong, and the owner fixes it. Across domains the same artifact would
assert a fact about domains that did not author it: it holds authority it
cannot legitimately have. So the estate gets **walks, never indexes** — the
mechanism (enumerate, batch, report) with the authority (own, assert, persist)
stripped off.

## The Test It Gives You

At any boundary crossing, ask of each primitive: **what does this thing know,
and who is entitled to know it?** Whatever survives that question is the part
you build. If a proposed mechanism requires one side to hold a fact the other
side owns — a consumer list, a membership register, a shared identity — the
authority half has leaked across and the design is wrong, however useful.

This is why every shipped estate mechanism reduces to *a read the consumer
could have made alone*: batching adds convenience, never information. And it
is why "one owner, everyone else imports" replaced a shared cross-domain work
id — same coordination, no borrowed authority.

## Why It Generalises

The framework has more boundaries coming: a public face, a third-party
consumer, a non-git substrate, collaborators inside one domain. Each will
present the same temptation — the primitive worked *here*, carry it whole.
The split is the discipline that keeps loose coupling from eroding one
reasonable-looking convenience at a time.
