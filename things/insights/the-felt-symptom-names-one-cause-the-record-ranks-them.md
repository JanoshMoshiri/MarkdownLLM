# The Felt Symptom Names One Cause; The Record Ranks Them

## The Insight

A felt problem arrives with a remedy already attached — the remedy for the
cause that can be seen from where the problem was felt. On 2026-10-09 the
operator, tripped by detached heads while four agents took turns in one
workspace from two clones, proposed a branch per conversation. It was a
sound remedy for the cause visible from inside a turn: conversations
colliding. It was ruled and planned within a day.

When he then asked for the evidence rather than anyone's view (2026-10-14),
one pinned read of the workspace's whole history ranked the causes, and the
one the remedy addressed came fourth of four. Ahead of it: two clones on
different framework versions rewriting one generated block in turns; turns
committed and never published; two agents in one working copy. None was
visible from inside a turn, and all three were invisible in the record
itself, because every agent committed under one identity. The remedy was
right for its cause. Its cause was the smallest.

## Why It Matters

A mechanism built from the felt symptom fixes what was seen and leaves the
larger causes standing — and by quieting the symptom it makes them harder
to find (`partial-coverage-quiets-the-uncovered-steps`). The record usually
holds the ranking already. Reading it is cheaper than the build, and much
cheaper than the build being aimed at the fourth cause.

## How To Apply

When a felt problem arrives with its remedy attached: record the ruling,
then read the record for the ranked causes before building past Phase 0 —
one pinned commit, independent readers per question, numbers and limits
stated. Build in the order the record ranks, each cause in the place it
lives (the seat went to the substrate, the loop rewrite to the workspace,
the branch plan behind both). The operator's leanings stay his rulings; the
evidence orders the work.

Dismissal condition: dismiss if two later felt problems are each found, by
such a read, to have come with a remedy for their largest cause — the read
then costs more than it finds.
