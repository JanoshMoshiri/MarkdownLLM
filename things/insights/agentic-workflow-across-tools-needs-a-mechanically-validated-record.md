# An Agentic Workflow Across Many Tools Needs A Mechanically Validated Record

## The Insight

The problem the framework exists to solve is the one every business with
agentic tools now meets. Work has to move between agents of different vendors
and between surfaces, in a way that is repeatable, structured and audited,
while each agent stays free to solve its own part. What carries the work is a
record every agent can reach, structured and validated mechanically and not
by the agent. Continuity between one person's sessions is one case of it;
one agent handing work to another is the same case.

The operator, 2026-11-23, in the operator's own words:

> "And the problem is, if I have all these agentic tools, how do I now create
> a workflow that can use these agentic tools, that uses all these different,
> what's the word, technologies, different surfaces, and have that
> continuity?"

On what an agentic workflow is:

> "What I understand that as it's a business model that leverages the
> non-deterministic compute of agent capability to do the jobs within the
> workflow that lend themselves for that type of work, but still in a
> repeatable, structured, and well-audited way."

On what carries it:

> "So for me, it's not just about continuity between work sessions, but it's
> about creating a underlying substrate, a way of storing information that's
> accessible by the agent in a way that's structured and validated, not by
> the agent, but mechanically."

On agents of any vendor working together:

> "It comes back to that problem of how do I now have my agents work
> together, whether they are a Claude agent or whether they are a Gemini
> agent, or whether it's an OpenClaw, you know, or whether it's any other
> that can operate, use some tools, create some outputs, maybe some files,
> record what happened, hand over, it can be picked up by the other one"

On how the work is held to its result:

> "the sensible way is not to have a very you know, stringent procedure that
> is followed by the agent, but we let the agent do what it's good at, which
> is solve the problem, but we have a test at the end which is perfect, that
> enables us to be able to get our end result exactly the way we want it to."

And on why the question has to be in the record at all:

> "You know, no matter how strong the model is or how clever it is, if it's
> not been given the correct prompt or asked the specific question you're
> trying to solve, you're not going to get the answer you want."

## What The Session Had Read Too Narrowly

The session narrowed the purpose three times before this
statement: first to a question of whether review by a second vendor catches
more, then to whether a structured record beats a good prompt, then to
continuity between one person's sessions. Each reading answered the question
in front of it and missed the problem behind it. The operator's point is that
this is the problem itself: a capable model given the wrong question returns
the wrong answer, so the question has to live somewhere every agent starts
from.

## The Session's Reading

These are the session's, offered for the operator's ruling, and none is
decided here.

1. **Agents are interchangeable clients of the record.** A handoff is a write
   the floor accepts; a pickup is a read. Agent-to-agent communication is not
   a separate messaging problem laid over the record.
2. **The gate has to sit at the record, not in one vendor's harness.** Part of
   the floor today rides one harness's hook. "Validated, not by the agent"
   has to mean "not by the agent's harness either": the check belongs where
   every agent must write to be seen.
3. **The test at the end is the handoff contract.** Each step declares what it
   must produce. The floor checks what can be checked mechanically: form,
   references, required parts, pins, whether the step ran. Whether the result
   is good is a named person's decision, or a second agent's review. No
   mechanical test is perfect about quality, and the contract should say
   which clauses are which.
4. **The record's value is the contract and the floor, more than the file
   format.** Markdown under git is how the record is held today. A business
   would also need access control and many writers at once, which this form
   does not supply.
5. **The entry contract states the mechanism and not the problem.** A session
   that starts from `AGENTS.md` learns how the system is built and is not told
   what it is for in these terms.

The operator named one vendor's offering as an answer to the same problem: a
layer that lets that vendor's agents read a business's data with its meaning
attached. The session's understanding, from general knowledge and not checked
on the day, is that such layers serve reading business data inside one
vendor's estate, and that the state of work in flight across vendors is the
part they leave open. That comparison is unverified.

## Why It Matters

It sets the weight of the forward plan without changing its principle. Three
phases of the conformance roadmap become its centre: the gauges that sit at
the remote and reach every surface, the handoff contract between domains, and
the proof of two agents of different vendors working one contract. The
operator's own example of that proof is a story written with one vendor's
agent and picked up by another's to make the video.
