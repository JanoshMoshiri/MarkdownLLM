# A Stated Dismissal Condition Needs A Reader

## The Insight

The `keep-active` marker asks for a reason, and the corpus answers it well —
roughly thirty active insights carry precise, falsifiable conditions for their
own retirement: *dismiss when the checks land and one cold read returns no
fix-residue findings*, *promote when a second sweep confirms the cadence*,
*re-open if variance persists across further sessions*. They are the corpus at
its most disciplined.

Nothing reads them. Not session-end, which forces a disposition on insights the
floor flags as orphaned but never re-tests the ones already exempted by a stated
reason. Not the retrospective, whose triage beat walks the backlog for
disposition but has no step that asks *which of these conditions has since come
true*. Not the floor, which reads the marker's presence and the reason's
existence but cannot judge its content.

So a `disposition_reason` is a **trigger in everything but syntax**: a condition,
written down, watched by no one. Several in the corpus are already satisfied or
nearly so, and their insights remain `active` — not because the judgement is
hard, but because no beat asks the question.

## Why It Matters

The keep-active marker was introduced as the brake that stops the orphan check
nagging about insights already reckoned with. It works — and in working it
created a quiet exemption class that only grows, because the exemption is
granted once and never revisited. That is the same shape as the drift the marker
was invented to prevent, moved one level up.

Two candidate routes, neither taken here: give the retrospective's triage beat
an explicit *conditions-met* pass over the exempted set — cheap, semantic, no
new mechanism; or, where a condition names a date or an observable committed
fact, write it as an actual dated trigger beside the prose, which is the
framework's own answer to human-gated waits applied to its own memory.

## Context

Surfaced by the independent substrate review of 2026-08-20 while auditing the
insight corpus: ninety-six active insights, sixty with no disposition at all,
and about thirty with conditions no beat re-reads. Recorded as an observation
rather than a design, because which of the two routes is right depends on how
many conditions turn out to be mechanically datable — a question the next
triage pass answers as a side effect of running.
