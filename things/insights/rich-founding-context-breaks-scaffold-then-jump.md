# Rich founding context breaks the scaffold-then-jump discipline

## The Insight

The `pre-domain-scaffold:isolate` mechanism pairs with an implicit workflow: scaffold a
domain from the framework, then **jump straight into the new domain** to author its
semantic half. That workflow silently assumes the domain's *founding context* is thin —
a name, a purpose, a schema sketch — light enough to survive the jump into a cold session.

Some domains are not born that way. They are born from a rich reasoning conversation: the
spine discovered, the taxonomy reasoned out, the lenses distilled — all held **in the
session**, not yet in any thing. Jumping into a cold domain session would force that
context through a lossy summary *before* it becomes things, which is exactly the
interpretation loss the framework exists to avoid. So a context-rich domain must be
**authored from the framework session where its founding context lives**, and only jumped
into once the founding things are committed.

The resolution is the general one: **the durable artifact should *be* the work, produced
where the context is hot** — not a summary of the intent to do the work, reconstructed
later. Write the spine, the surfaces, the lenses as things while the understanding is
live; the committed things are then the lossless handoff, and the cold session reads
reality, not a reconstruction.

## Why It Matters

It names a real exception to the default birth workflow and the reason for it. The
scaffold-then-jump path is right for the common, thin case; it is a trap for the
context-rich case, where obeying it manufactures the very loss the discipline is meant to
prevent. Candidate framework follow-up (the operator's to weigh): explicit guidance — or a
scaffold affordance — for context-rich births, so the exception is documented rather than
rediscovered each time.

## Provenance

Surfaced 2026-07-01 during the birth of the `agent-architect` domain, which was authored
end to end (schema, spine method, seven surfaces, an insight, a principle, eight lenses)
from the framework session because its founding reasoning could not survive a jump. The
operator's observation — that this "goes against the discipline I put in place" of
scaffold-then-jump, and is "something to ponder for future complex domain creation" —
sharpened into the account above. `source: both`, `origin: stated`.
