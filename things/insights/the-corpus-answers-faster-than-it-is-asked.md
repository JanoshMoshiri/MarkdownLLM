# The Corpus Answers Faster Than It Is Asked

## The Insight

In a mature corpus, the ratio between *questions arriving* and *rulings
needed* stops being one-to-one and becomes heavily skewed — because most
arriving questions are already answered by something written for a different
reason, months earlier.

The period's counts, all from 2026-09-12/13:

- An external review raised **seven** boundary criticisms. **Five** were
  already insights in the corpus, dated, with evidence. Two were new.
- A backlog of **twenty-seven** un-walked modifications, plus one held cue,
  traced to **four** rulings already on the record — each sealed by the
  session that made it, months or weeks before the carrier existed to hold
  the verdicts.
- Two direction questions (which product surface; how to automate the
  retrospective) were answered by reading the corpus rather than by taste —
  in the second case the record answered *against* the literal proposal and
  *for* its intent, which is the harder and more useful direction.
- The one open conflict was ruled on a proposal that had been written
  seventeen days earlier and had not moved since.

## Why It Matters

- **It changes what the scarce act is.** Not answering — *ruling*. A ruling is
  the thing that covers many pending questions at once; answering them
  individually is the expensive path the operator instinctively refused when
  faced with twenty-eight cues ("you make the decision… lead by the
  evidence"). The framework's job is then to make each ruling *reachable* from
  everywhere it applies, which is what a citation edge is.
- **It reframes what the record is for.** Not retrieval — *compounding*. A
  decision written to settle one thing is read later by work that did not
  exist when it was written. That only pays if the reading is cheap, which is
  the whole argument for structure over prose.
- **It sets an honest measurement.** Ratio of questions-the-corpus-answered to
  questions-needing-a-fresh-ruling, per period. If it rises, the record is
  compounding; if it falls while the corpus grows, the record is merely
  accumulating — and accumulation without compounding is the failure mode
  every knowledge base dies of.

## Context

Observed at the close of the 2026-09-12/13 arc, when the same shape appeared
four times in two days from four unrelated directions.

**The caution is load-bearing and stated in full:** this is the framework's own
corpus, with its author present, over a period deliberately spent working on
the corpus's own machinery — the most favourable possible conditions for the
record to have the answer. `the-root-is-not-a-representative-domain` applies
directly. What would make this real is the same ratio from a domain that is not
the root, run by someone who did not write the record.
