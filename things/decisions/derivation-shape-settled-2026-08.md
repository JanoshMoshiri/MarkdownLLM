# Decision: The Derivation Shape Is Settled — Declaration, Not Rebuild

Made by the operator in session, 2026-08-27, on the worked QMS specimen and
the four questions answered in the brief. The operator's verdict, given in
his own words: **the domain holds this discretion already.**

## What is settled

**Reconciliation is declaration, not rebuild.** A definition that already
realises the atom's seven decisions is reconciled by *saying so*, in its own
body, against the framework version its domain already pins. The QMS specimen
demonstrated that a domain converges on the atom's shape without being told
to; the distance to full specialisation was one section, not a restructure.

**The declaration is corpus-local.** There is no edge to add in a domain: the
referential check makes an out-of-corpus `linked_things` target an Error, and
foundation reaches a domain by refresh — the porch is explicitly the wrong
channel. So a domain definition keeps its existing edge to the domain's own
workflow skill and carries the derivation as a body section. Only a
framework-root definition can carry the `implements` edge to the atom
directly, because it shares the corpus (`substrate-floor-development` already
does). **The root is not a representative domain** — a pattern authored and
validated there can be invalid in every domain, and this one was.

**Three routes, chosen per definition by the domain:**

- **Declare** — the mapping succeeds: write the derivation section, name the
  collapses. The default.
- **Repair, then declare** — the mapping fails (a decision no stage realises).
  The gap is a process finding; restructuring repairs the process, never the
  names.
- **Retire** — zero runs and no live demand: retire honestly with the
  reasoning recorded, rather than keeping dead vocabulary.

## What stays the domain's

Mapping depth, per its own lenses — a regulated domain's regulator lens
forces all seven decisions named and the collapses stated; a low-stakes domain
may map proportionately under the atom's own working rule. The retire-or-run
verdict on any zero-run definition. Both by membrane doctrine: the substrate
ships the pattern and the question; the verdict is local, and the vantage
never becomes everyone's editor.

## What the framework does

Ship the worked example (a `type: example` thing at the root, derived from
the ratified QMS pass) and a pointer step in `domain-refresh.md`, so the
pattern travels the channel foundation already uses. Additive to a draft and
an evolving spec — it flows under the gates census's row 15, with the root
push as the review seat.

## The hold, lifted

`workflow-reconciliation-precedes-new-definitions` blocked minting new
definitions until this shape was settled. It is settled; the hold is spent.
New definitions may be minted following the shape above. Sequencing beyond
that is a matter for `estate-workflow-derivation`, which carries the
operator's declared gate.
