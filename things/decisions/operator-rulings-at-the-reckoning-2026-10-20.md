# Decision: Three rulings at the 2026-10-20 reckoning — disarm the estate chase, narrow the cue trigger, leave the plan carrier

## Context

The operator asked, on 2026-10-20, for everything that did not need his
ruling to be closed from the evidence the things hold, and for what the
evidence could not settle to come to him through the native choice prompt —
the prompt the change-reconciliation walk uses for its residue. The agent
settled thirty decisions by citation (`close:` commit 379f497: eleven plans
paused, three completed, three cancelled, three insights promoted, seven held,
one trigger answered, nine cues answered). Three questions remained that the
record could not settle because each changes what the record means or who
reads it.

## Inputs Considered

- `framework-retrospective-2026-10` — its open questions: whether `plan`
  should carry `substrate-floor-development`, and whether the cue exhaust is
  felt (173 cues in five weeks, 78% ruled not-inflection).
- `estate-retrospective-synthesis-2026-08` — its time trigger, armed
  2026-08-27 and re-dated 2026-09-03, 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-17, fired a
  fourth time on 2026-10-18 with two questions left: whether the regulated
  cluster's own estate retrospective consumed the synthesis (only that
  workspace's record knows) and whether the standing aggregation read is ruled
  (`mdllm reckon` has done the per-workspace half since 2026-10-05).
- The day's own evidence: nine cues raised on 2026-10-20, all not-inflection;
  six of them raised by an authorship label, a verified flip, or a phase
  appended — changes the doctrine already calls disposition-only.

## Options

1. **The estate chase.** Disarm the trigger (its halves have found their
   readers); run the estate retrospective (a session's work on the membrane,
   consuming the dispatcher digests); or re-date a fifth time (the pattern the
   retrospective named as the chase).
2. **The cue trigger.** Narrow it so disposition-only and label-only changes
   raise no cue (a floor change); or leave it raising on every modification and
   keep the exhaust as the audit trail.
3. **The plan carrier.** Leave `substrate-floor-development` for explicit
   runs and let the root's process stay a commit-stream shape; or bind `plan`
   as its carrier, making every plan a run with stage membership enforced — a
   change of meaning on about forty plans.

## Decision

The operator, through the native prompt, 2026-10-20:

1. **Disarm it.** The per-workspace half of the aggregation read is
   mechanical now; the regulated half is chased in its own workspace. The
   trigger stops firing at the root; the synthesis stands as the August
   record.
2. **Narrow it.** A change that touches only disposition, provenance or
   authorship fields, or only appends a dated postscript, owes no cue. The
   build is proposed as its own session; the cue-carrier plan records the
   precision before and after.
3. **Leave it.** The root's process stays a commit-stream shape; the
   definition is used only for explicit runs.

## Consequences

- `estate-retrospective-synthesis-2026-08` 1.2 carries no trigger; its body
  records the disarming and the ruling.
- The cue-trigger narrowing is a floor change owned by a proposed session;
  until it lands, label-only changes still raise cues and are answered by
  citation.
- The plan-carrier question is closed as answered, not deferred; a future
  reopening needs new evidence — a root arc that could not be read from the
  commit stream.
- The reckoning's residue band was exercised as designed: thirty settled by
