# Decision: Enshrine the Operating-Scope Recognitions (Manifesto v2.8)

## Context

A deliberate conversation (2026-08-06), opened by the operator from the
assistant-register Phase 0 drives, surfaced three connected recognitions about
what the substrate has turned out to be in use: use-time programming
(`operating-is-programming`), the discipline prosthesis serving both
processors (`the-substrate-is-a-discipline-prosthesis`), and authority rather
than expertise as the operator requirement
(`expertise-is-an-output-not-a-prerequisite`). The operator declared the
capture an inflection and asked for the full change-reconciliation pass. The
question: enshrine the recognitions, and in what form?

## Inputs Considered

- The three canonical insights (`informed_by`, pinned), each carrying its own
  evidence and honest boundary — including the builder's-curse caveat on the
  everyday-scope claim.
- The v2.6 precedent (`divergence-primitive-promotion`): a recognition earns
  manifesto altitude only by clarifying, never by adding a layer; the
  acceptance test is *is there anything new an agent must do?*
- The reconciliation Walk over the manifesto's 31 declared inbound edges plus
  the literal and conceptual tiers: no dependent contradicted — the specs'
  "expert" usage reads correctly under the authority framing, and manifesto
  §The System as Collaborator already carried the expertise-compounds claim
  in embryo.

## Options

1. **Insights only, no manifesto change.** Rejected: the recognitions revise
   the manifesto's own scope story ("expert" framing, the unspecified
   processor, the missing operator-side discovery symmetry) — leaving the
   manifesto silent would be the model not naming what reality shows, the
   exact divergence route 2 exists for.
2. **A new spec or section-per-insight.** Rejected: bolt-on ceremony; the
   material is rationale and scope, not operative rules — no kernel content,
   no new mechanism.
3. **Recognitions woven into the manifesto's existing sections, zero new
   mechanism (chosen).** Four additions, each beside the passage it extends:
   *The Second Collapse: Operation Is Programming* (after the notation
   continuity); the *which processor* scope note (inside The Thesis); the
   operator-side discovery symmetry (inside Discovery); *The Operator:
   Authority, Not Credentials* (inside The Generalization). README refreshed
   to carry the scope in its landing prose.

## Decision

Take option 3, by route 2 of the divergence primitive: revise the model with
recorded rationale, through the framework's own change process. Manifesto
v2.7 → v2.8. Acceptance test passed: after promotion there is nothing new an
agent must *do* — the kernel is untouched, no hook, no check, no field. The
only change is that what the substrate demonstrably is in use — programmable
by its operator, disciplined on the operator's behalf, operated on authority
— is now named where the paradigm is stated.

The operator made the call; the reasoning was developed jointly across the
conversation that produced the trilogy.

## Consequences

- The manifesto carries the three recognitions (v2.8); the canonical insights
  stay `active` with `promoted_to` set, cited from the sections they ground.
- The parked adjacent question stays parked: the register/orientation as a
  domain-configurable surface is spec-when-foreseeable material, constrained
  by the what-to-say/how-to-speak line — nothing is felt yet.
- The teaching claim ("the manual is the conversation") makes the
  assistant-register plan load-bearing for the manifesto's discovery story,
  not cosmetic — its Phase 5 decay watch inherits that weight.
- Reversible: the enshrinement is the harvest commit plus this seal; revert
  restores manifesto v2.7 and drops the edges, losing nothing but the names.
