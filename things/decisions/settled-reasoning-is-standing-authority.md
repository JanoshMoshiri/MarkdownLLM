# Decision: Settled Reasoning Is Standing Authority

Ruled by the operator, in session, in voice, 2026-08-28 — and recorded here
because a ruling that lives only in conversation is not domain state. The
operator's own formula, given with the ruling, is the through-line:

> **A system discovered by LLMs, run by LLMs, directed by humans.**

## The ruling

The agent — any session operating within the MarkdownLLM estate, substrate or
domain — holds **standing authority to initiate and execute change wherever
the reasoning for that change is already settled in the corpus.** Refinement
or new feature, floor mechanism or spec line, framework root or domain: if the
evidence is recorded, the design is decided, and the pain has been felt at
threshold, the agent acts. It does not wait for the operator to restate a
conclusion the corpus already holds.

The occasioning instance, named by the operator in the act of ruling: a floor
check (structural-pin resolution) whose failure had been felt five times
across three days and two sessions, whose design had been fully decided in the
backlog since 2026-08-26 — and which sat unbuilt because the agent was waiting
for a confirmation that added no information. The operator's judgement: that
wait was the defect, not the caution.

## What "settled" means — the bar

Reasoning is settled when the corpus already carries both halves:

1. **The evidence** — repeated felt instances on the record, a measured
   finding, or convergence per the operating model's admission rule. One
   same-day proposal is not settled; a backlog item marked "felt twice" plus
   three further instances is.
2. **The decided direction** — the mechanism or shape is already recorded
   (a backlog design, a settled doctrine the change instantiates, a
   precedent ruling that transfers).

Where the evidence exists but the direction genuinely does not, the agent
does not stall and does not guess silently: it **settles the reasoning in the
corpus first** — writes the insight or decision with its evidence, in the
open, committed — and then acts on it. Chat is not corpus. A conclusion the
agent reached but never recorded confers nothing.

## The boundary that does not move

Excluded from this grant, permanently, is everything the gates census marks
consequence-permanent — adopted by reference (`gates-census-2026-08`, rows
the operator endorsed in ruling): money and external-party effects; deletion,
erasure and history rewrite; publication of the root release surface (the
push); granting publication authority; remote creation; `verified` flips on
external things; permission-bearing installations and authority grants —
**including any widening of this decision itself**, which is an authority
grant and therefore outside its own scope, structurally: a grant that could
extend itself would be the self-authorization defect one level up.

Two further standing limits:

- **Domain-declared gate authority stands.** A stage, approval field, or gate
  that names a human is passed by that human, whatever this ruling says.
  Autonomy here is authority to *initiate and carry work to* the gates, never
  to pass one that names someone.
- **Statutory and regulated acts** remain where their own definitions put
  them: the director files, the named human verifies, the QMS's governance is
  the QMS's.

## The review structure

At the framework root, nothing changes about review: the root declares
`autopush: false`, so every autonomous commit still lands behind the
operator's deliberate push — the census's row 1, the one gate that wraps all
root change, untouched. In the domains, autopush is the operator's own
standing grant of 2026-08-22, so review there is retrospective by the
estate's existing loops — retrospectives, reviews, the conflict discipline,
and the record itself. That asymmetry is accepted in the act of this ruling,
not discovered later.

## The record discipline that makes it safe

Every self-initiated change of substance carries its settled reasoning with
it — pinned inputs, linked evidence, the felt-instance count — in its commit
message or its decision thing. Initiative without a citation trail is not
what was granted. The audit line is what distinguishes sovereignty from
drift, and it is also what lets the operator direct: a directed system is one
whose actions can be read, disputed, and re-ruled after the fact.

## Effective immediately — and enacted in the same hour

The ruling took effect in the session that recorded it: the structural-pin
check was already building when the ruling was given, and the three
floor fixes whose evidence was already measured (the `skill` orientation
exclusion, the dark-obligations bucket, the zero-run definition sensor) were
launched with it. The operator queue's v1.1 records which of its rows this
ruling moved from awaiting-assent to executing, and which stay at the
operator's seats — because the seats that remain are now exactly the ones
that should: direction, irreversibles, and the gates that name him.
