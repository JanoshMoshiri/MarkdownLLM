# Decision: the substrate reconciles its contradictions, and restated facts get one owner

**Decided by:** the operator (cue and scope, voice, 2026-08-09) and the agent
(the walk), after two 2026-08-08 field sessions in a layered harness surfaced
defects that a full substrate sweep then traced to the framework root itself.

## The question

Two domain sessions misbehaved in the same week — an orientation the operator
could not follow, and a write made without the workflow skill's authorisation
step. Both sessions' agents had **complied** with the reading lists and
generated blocks they were handed. Was the defect in the bootstrap plugin
that handed them over, or in the substrate the plugin faithfully reproduced?

## The finding

The substrate. A full sweep (every spec, guide, template, generator, and the
tool surfaces the specs make claims about) found the framework holding
**contradictory contracts simultaneously**: git-workflow.md asserting both
autopush and human-only push; orchestration.md declaring no inherited
bindings while the generator installs four; five competing startup
sequences; a hard-hook count wrong in the guide's template text; three
reserved-type lists each differently incomplete; prompt templates teaching a
condition the spec removed two releases earlier — and beneath the prose, two
floor defects: the orient view labelling 30-day look-aheads "Triggers
fired", and the session gate crying wolf at every fresh gated clone.

## The decision

1. **Reconcile at the source, not the copy.** Every contradiction was fixed
   in the surface that owns the fact; copies now defer explicitly (the
   tool's `RESERVED_STATUSES`, the sentinel's `foundational_specs`, the
   generated managed blocks, the generator's tier routing).
2. **Restated facts get one owner.** Counts, lists, sequences, and doctrine
   restated in authored prose are the recurring drift class — three of them
   had drifted *again* after being corrected once. Where a restatement could
   not be deleted, it now names its authority and yields to it on
   disagreement.
3. **The routing gap closes in the generator.** `prompts/` is routed by the
   tier-routing block from the filesystem, with the derived-reading-list
   rule stated inline; the specification and write skills are named required
   reading before any write. Every reading list derived from a domain kernel
   now carries what the Session Start ritual names.
4. **The floor stops lying about its own output.** Fired and upcoming are
   separate buckets end to end; the gate's no-attestation finding names the
   fresh-clone reading and `doctor` attributes a gate-only block as setup
   ordering. Enforcement is unchanged — only the labels were false.
5. **The re-open condition is spent here, not on enforcement.**
   `pretooluse-action-boundary-gate` stays parked: the two field misses were
   compliance with a contradictory contract, so the rightward move this
   evidence buys is derivation, not a gate. The next same-class miss against
   the now-consistent contract is what buys the gate.

## Consequences

- Every scaffolded domain shows domain-kernel DRIFT at its next coherence
  run until it regenerates (`mdllm domain-kernel .`) — the refresh path
  working as designed; the v3.30.0 refresh backfill entry names it.
- Domains born before v3.30.0 should re-adopt the changed prompt templates
  (fired/upcoming, import triggers, terminal-status wording).
- Two porch insights in a consumer domain (a handoff list replacing its
  contract; a check running before its own precondition) have their
  dismissal conditions substantially met by this reconciliation — their
  disposition is that domain's call at its next session, not this repo's.
- Exposed, because consumer domains' extent reviews and parked insights rest
  on exactly this fact: which substrate defects were fixed, and at which
  version.
