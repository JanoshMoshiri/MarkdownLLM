# Framework Retrospective — early August 2026: The Cadence Week

The seven days after `framework-retrospective-2026-07`: v3.24.0 → v3.26.0,
36 commits, three releases in four working days. The through-line: **the
framework spent July learning that its laws apply between domains; it spent
this week learning that they apply to itself** — its own definition
surfaces (3.24), its own arithmetic (3.25), and its own publication and
reconciliation discipline (3.26). This retrospective is itself the new
doctrine's first act: the reflective beat run deliberately *before* the
release is pushed public, on the operator's own initiative — the walk
earning the publish.

## What We Were Trying To Do

Close the gap the 2026-08-01 estate sweep exposed: the framework routed
divergences diligently on the data axis and had almost no sensors on its
fractal claim that definitions are things too. Then, mid-arc, absorb the
operator's dimension-shift finding — multi-domain work within a day made
publication-as-deliberate-act and retrospection-bound-to-memory stop
carrying — without inventing any new primitive to do it.

## What Worked

- **The sensors caught their own builders, twice, within days of
  shipping.** The disclosure boundary blocked this agent's first plan
  commit (private domain names in a public-root thing); the cue-candidate
  advisory's first-ever firing was on the v3.26.0 release commit itself,
  flagging the two modified definition surfaces the walk had just covered.
  A control whose first catch is its own builder is evidence it fires on
  contact, not on ceremony.
- **The generated-surface collapse was demonstrated, not asserted.** The
  autopush walk found ~15 restatements of the push doctrine; 13 reconciled
  through one generator string plus regeneration. The cost model
  (`a-generated-surface-collapses-its-walk`) now has a measured first
  instance.
- **Deploy-when-felt held its shape.** The v3.22.0 autopush hold named its
  release condition; the condition arrived (in a different costume — the
  operator as his own collaborator) and was recognised, argued from
  evidence (a day of repeated overrides), ruled attributably, and lifted
  the same day. The hold system is doing what it was designed to do:
  deferring without forgetting.
- **The walk found the freshest possible touchpoint.** A skill authored
  three days earlier by the same builder now revising the doctrine carried
  the doctrine's restatement. Nothing but the textual tier would have
  found it.
- **Three releases, zero new primitives.** The estate retrospective, the
  cue question, the release walk, autopush — each is an existing primitive
  recognised at a radius it already governed. The discovery discipline
  held under speed.

## What Didn't Work

- **Retrospectives stopped happening, and this one is evidence of the
  disease as much as the cure.** It exists because the operator remembered
  — the clock that would have chased it shipped only hours before it was
  written. The v3.24.0 cadence sensor had been firing in `validate` for
  three days and reached no one; the operator's felt sense arrived first.
  Sensors that fire at the wrong moment are wallpaper.
- **A measurement artifact briefly reported drift as resolved.** During
  the estate sweeps, a re-grep matched the wrong word and reported
  template residue as zero estate-wide; the error was caught by re-running
  the sensor properly, but for one message the operator was told movement
  had happened that had not. `a-caveat-must-travel-with-the-conclusion-it-qualifies`
  applies to the framework's own operator as much as to any domain.
- **The conflict scan's affordability rests on an index the root has not
  deployed.** `detect-conflicts` scan mode leans on the `relationships`
  index; `mdllm index . check` reports it not deployed at the framework
  root, so full-edge sweeps fall back to Level 2 over 133 things. This
  retrospective's sweep was therefore period-scoped (the changed doctrine
  and its neighbours, plus mechanical phrase-sweeps) rather than
  exhaustive — honest scope, but the gap is the root's to close.
- **Three domains remain outside the kernel shape**, visible across three
  consecutive estate sweeps and still undone — parked without a recorded
  parking ruling, which is drift by this framework's own standard: the
  distinction between deferred and forgotten is a written ruling.

## Patterns We Noticed

- **The primitive goes fractal, and recognising it is cheaper than
  building it.** Session-end → domain retrospective → estate retrospective;
  cue question at commit → imports-check at membrane → clocks at epoch →
  walk at release. Each outward step this week cost a section in a spec and
  a trigger, not a mechanism.
- **Felt-before-measured is the reliable ignition.** All three releases
  began from operator felt-friction (hand-fixed drift classes; two domains
  hitting the same arithmetic wall; a day of push overrides) and were then
  verified by measurement before building. Neither half suffices alone.
- **The boundary between floor and human is the invariant every change
  preserved.** Across sensors, calc, autopush, advisories, clocks: detection
  mechanical, judgement human, verdicts never scored. The week added no
  exceptions — which is the strongest signal the boundary is drawn where
  reality wants it.

## What Should Change

1. **Deploy the `relationships` index at the framework root** — the
   conflict scan's affordability depends on it, and the root is now the
   corpus where high-fan-in edges matter most (thing.md: 31 inbound).
2. **Normalise the `source` field** (see schema coherence below) — one
   name currently carries three value-spaces.
3. **Rule on the three unkernelled domains** — adopt the kernel shape or
   record the parking ruling; either ends the drift, only one requires
   work.
4. **Consider closing `deterministic-calculation`** — see open questions.

## Open Questions Going Forward

## Reflexive Scans

**Conflict scan (scan mode, period-scoped + mechanical sweeps):** no
contradictions surfaced. The one live-surface hit of the superseded push
doctrine is the revision note that quotes it deliberately; historical plans
carrying the old line were walked and ruled consistent-as-history during
Phase 1, with the supersession recorded in
`autopush-moves-the-deliberate-act`. The premature-publish insight stands
reconciled via the release-surface carve-out. No open conflict things; no
new ones created. Scope caveat recorded under What Didn't Work.

**Schema coherence:** one genuine finding — the emergent field `source`
carries three distinct value-spaces: an enum of who-surfaced-it on insights
(`human`/`agent`/`both`/`build`/`operator`), a provenance domain on two
decisions (`code-architect` — duplicating the core `source_domain` concept
under a looser name), and long free-text session descriptions on
eval-derived insights (overlapping the separate `session` field's job).
This is the inverse of the drift the prompt hunts — one name, several
meanings — and the same class of incoherence. Proposed normalisation, for
ruling rather than mass edit: decisions' provenance domain moves to the
core triple's vocabulary; free-text descriptions split into `session`
(date) plus body prose; the insight enum keeps `source` as-is (45 uses,
consistent). No other convergent pairs found; `session` (4 types, one
meaning) and the `*_by` family (`decided_by`/`verified_by`/`resolved_by`)
are healthy emergence, not drift.
