# Framework Retrospective — August 2026: The Floor's Floor

The twenty-three days after `framework-retrospective-2026-08a`: v3.26 → v3.36,
eleven releases, 354 commits. The through-line: **the period before this one
taught the framework that its laws apply to itself; this period built the
enforcement that no longer needs the builder watching.** The session gate, the
kernel emitted rather than pointed at, three floor sprints run through the
framework's own workflow primitive, revision binding, and a guard that cannot
be turned on its author. A note on the id: the retrospective spec's natural id
for this period is occupied by the carrier plan that chased this document into
existence, which is itself part of the period's story.

## What We Were Trying To Do

Absorb what the estate threw at the substrate — a cross-harness integrity
breach, two independent substrate reviews in one day, a vendor-adapter matrix,
an eight-round review loop — and answer each with structure rather than
vigilance: the session gate (v3.28), session-start hardening (v3.33), the
floor sprints (v3.34–3.36), the universal workflow's promotion to foundation
(v3.35). The carrier plan names the cost honestly: every hour went into
mechanism, and the reflective beat is the one thing the mechanism cannot
perform on itself.

## What Worked

- **The cadence fix worked on its first firing.** This document exists because
  a dated chase built on 2026-08-20 fired on 2026-08-27 and a session obeyed
  it. The estate's rule — date the chase on a human-gated wait — applied at
  the radius that had been missing it.
- **The framework built its floor through its own primitives.**
  `substrate-floor-development` is a workflow-definition; sprints 1–3 and the
  seams run executed as workflow-runs with pinned definitions and floor-checked
  transitions; the seams work was then verified by an independent cold reader
  ("the review that had to review itself"). The dogfooding claim now covers
  the mechanism layer, not just the corpus.
- **Same-day breach closure held as the standard.** The
  `cowork-integrity-estate-sweep` found the harness had never handed over;
  the session gate shipped the same day, and CI caught the gate eating its own
  scaffolding on ship day. Sensors catching their builders is no longer an
  event; it is the routine.
- **Release-walk discipline held across eleven releases.** The previous period
  had to run a correction sweep over nine unwalked releases; this period's
  commit stream shows each release walked and sealed at the time.
- **08a's should-changes landed.** The relationships index is deployed at the
  root — and this retrospective's full-edge conflict scan, the first ever run
  at full width, is the direct payoff. The `source` normalisation on decisions
  was executed (with one residue, fixed below).
- **Publication authority went estate-wide without ever being granted by
  silence** (2026-08-22): autopush only on literal declaration, the release
  surface still the human's deliberate act.

## What Didn't Work

- **The cadence broke for three weeks before the fix could prove itself.** The
  volume and milestone triggers had fired repeatedly with no reader; only the
  dated chase reached a session. 08a already recorded that sensors firing at
  the wrong moment are wallpaper; the period proved it again before fixing it.
- **Tracking drift recurred inside the attention layer itself.**
  `mechanical-coherence-checks-backlog` sat `not-started` while floor-sprint-3
  shipped four of its items; an insight carried a `disposition_reason` with no
  `disposition`; one resolved conflict named its resolver and one did not. All
  fixed today; all instances of the class the corpus already named
  (`tracking-artifacts-can-drift-from-reality`), living in the layer whose job
  is tracking.
- **A high-confidence insight stood contradicting the entry contract for
  months.** `hard-hooks-require-observable-agent-caused-triggers` still
  asserts a two-hook hard set and excludes the harness-session class two
  current hard hooks occupy. Nothing noticed because scan-mode conflict
  detection had never run at full width until today. Routed as an open
  conflict for the operator.
- **Two structural pins were mistranscribed in one sprint** — the identifier
  form of the arithmetic lesson (`a-wrong-sum-is-indistinguishable-from-a-right-one`).
  The floor now resolves `definition_commit`; the pre-commit resolution of all
  pins is a named backlog candidate.
- **Two high-priority things stalled past the 21-day line:**
  `response-depth-control` (not-started, 26d) and `cohesiveness-sensors`
  (in-progress, 22d). Looks-active-is-not is precisely the drift the stall
  line exists to catch; both need a ruling or a schedule, not silence.

## Patterns We Noticed

- **Enforcement migrates rightward; the human boundary does not move.** Every
  hardening this period moved an anchor — interpretation → git-fs
  (session gate, revision binding, coherence legs) or → harness-session
  (adapters, emission) — and none moved judgement into the floor or scored a
  verdict. Zero exceptions across eleven releases remains the strongest
  signal the boundary is drawn where reality wants it.
- **The reflective beat is displaced by exactly the work it reflects on.** The
  displacement is structural, not moral: mechanism work satisfies the same
  builder instinct the reflection would. The countermeasure the period landed
  is to give every judgement beat a mechanical partner that chases — and the
  partner works, on the evidence of this document.
- **Capture-to-reckoning is rebalancing, not rebalanced.** 126 insights: 109
  active, 14 promoted, 3 dismissed. The period produced genuine
  condition-met promotions (the read gate; today the census insight). The
  first conditions-met census (below) found most stated conditions genuinely
  unmet — meaning the corpus's uniform keep-active answer was largely *true*,
  which is different from dead. The disease was never the verdict; it was
  that nothing re-read the conditions. It has a reader now.
- **The corpus has entered its own deferred ceiling.** 284 things against
  `thing-lifecycle.md`'s 200–300 window. The scale question stopped being
  theoretical this period; it is now a due ruling.
