# Run a 10k

## Desired Outcome

Finish an organised 10k race before mid-October without stopping to walk. The
specific race is the Riverside Autumn 10k on 2026-10-04 — entry not yet booked,
which becomes a task once training survives a full month.

## Why This Matters

Two years of desk work have eroded a fitness base that used to exist. A race
with a date on it converts "should run more" into a training plan with a
deadline. The recurring Tuesday run (`recurring-tuesday-run`) is the engine;
this goal is what it drives toward.

## Progress So Far

- Week 1–2: two 3k runs completed, slower than hoped, knees fine.
- `target_date` is an emergent field on this thing — the schema grew it
  because a goal with a race needs one; no framework change was required.

## Success Criteria

Crossing the finish line. Time is explicitly not a criterion for the first one.
